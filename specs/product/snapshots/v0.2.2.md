# Product snapshot — v0.2.2 (2026-08-08)

Current state of the product after the distributed rework. Refreshed after
every release; this file is the "what exists today" answer to product
questions.

## In one sentence

A deterministic, zero-dependency distributed fingerprint computation engine in
Zig 0.14.1: a TypeScript browser SDK collects 102 signals and POSTs a versioned
SignalPackage to an ingress; stateless Zig workers (Docker containers) compute
the canonical fingerprint, risk, and entropy; results fan out over AMQP to the
fraud platform (Go, separate repo).

## Testing posture

- `zig build test --summary all` → 383 tests (376 unit + 7 integration/e2e).
- Golden fixture `signal-package-v2.bin` pins the canonical digest
  (`db29fc13…e6c75`, features=3, schema=2) across Zig, worker, and TS tests.
- Parity tests cross-check the TS serializer against the Zig codec.
